## 3. Greenhouse Gas Exchange Dynamics & Spatial Heterogeneity

### 3.1 Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$ / NEE / Reco / GPP)
- **Net Ecosystem Exchange (NEE):** Tower observations show active photosynthetic uptake ($\text{GPP}$) during the growing season offsetting ecosystem respiration ($\text{R}_{\text{eco}}$).
- **Gap-Filling Depth Dependency:** Gap-filling cumulative NEE divergence is heavily dependent on soil temperature sensor depth. Using shallow ($5\text{ cm}$) vs. deep ($50\text{ cm}$) temperature proxies alters cumulative annual sign/magnitude, highlighting the necessity of deep temperature calibration in peat soils.
- **Chamber Respiration:** Strong exponential temperature relationship; no significant difference between $16\text{ cm}$ vs. $42\text{ cm}$ chamber heights, confirming volume-geometric corrections.

### 3.2 Methane ($\text{CH}_4$) Fluxes
- **Spatial Differentiation:**
  - **East Trench (Chambers 1–2):** Consistent net methane sink or near-neutral flux ($\approx -0.03\text{ nmol m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$).
  - **Central Site (Chambers 3–7):** Strong net emission hotspot ($\le 42\text{ nmol m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$ in summer), driven by submerged organic matter and elevated water tables.
  - **West Trench (Chamber 8):** Highest temporal variability with episodic ebullition/pulses.
- **Seasonality:** Summer peak coincides with maximum soil temperatures and water table drawdowns creating active oxic-anoxic boundary layers.

### 3.3 Nitrous Oxide ($\text{N}_2\text{O}$)
- Highest fluxes concentrated in the Central Site during autumn wetting events following prolonged summer periods, driven by nitrification-denitrification couplets at fluctuating water tables.

## 4. Climatic Radiative Forcing & Global Warming Potential (GWP)

| Metric | 100-Year Time Horizon ($\text{GWP}_{100}$) | 20-Year Time Horizon ($\text{GWP}_{20}$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\text{CH}_4$ Multiplier** | $\times 28 - 29.8$ | $\times 82.5 - 84$ |
| **$\text{N}_2\text{O}$ Multiplier** | $\times 265 - 273$ | $\times 264 - 268$ |
| **Systemic Climate Verdict** | **Moderate Climate Neutrality / Sink-to-Source transition** | **Acute Net Positive Warming Source** |

### The Core Thesis Paradigm Shift:
1. **$\text{CO}_2$-Only Vision:** Looking solely at carbon dioxide presents an apparent carbon sink during active vegetative regrowth.
2. **Full Multi-Gas Vision ($\text{CO}_2 + \text{CH}_4 + \text{N}_2\text{O}$):** The massive radiative power of front-loaded methane pulses completely reverses the net balance, especially under the 20-year horizon ($\text{GWP}_{20}$).
3. **Management Consequence:** Rewetting without managing labile organic inputs and trench microtopography creates substantial short-term atmospheric warming before long-term peat accumulation benefits are realized.
